Brewing Tips

Essential knowledge to transform your coffee game

Water Quality Matters

Use filtered water at 195-205°F. Bad water = bad coffee, no matter how good your beans are.

Measure Your Beans

Aim for a 1:15 coffee-to-water ratio. A kitchen scale is a barista's best friend.

Freshness is Key

Grind just before brewing. Use beans within 2-4 weeks of roasting for peak flavor.
